# CMO — Marketing Strategy

Demand generation, brand, content, channels, and measurement. Extends the
[GTM Marketing Playbook](../gtm/MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md) (website copy, landing pages,
pitch deck, one-pager, ROI calculator, launch campaign) with SEO, a content
calendar, channel strategy, referral, and the measurement model.

## Brand positioning

- **Category:** the **Experience Commerce Platform** — "Sell tickets anywhere, get
  paid everywhere, in 15 minutes."
- **Pillars:** (1) time-to-value, (2) payments/payouts, (3) your brand + your data,
  (4) movies **and** events, (5) transparent fees.
- **Tone:** organizer-first, plain-spoken, anti-aggregator, proof-driven.

## Website improvements (priorities)

1. Hero + 3 value props + proof strip (per Marketing Playbook copy).
2. **Segment landing pages** (/colleges, /cinemas, /conferences, /music, /comedy,
   /sports, /nonprofits, /eventbrite-alternative).
3. **Interactive ROI/fee calculator** (lead magnet).
4. **Case-study hub** + logo wall + testimonials.
5. **Pricing page** (transparent, calculator-linked).
6. **Trust page** (security, uptime, data ownership).
7. Fast, mobile-first, localized currency; clear dual CTA (Start free / Book a demo).
8. Analytics + conversion instrumentation on every CTA.

## SEO strategy

- **Money keywords (comparison intent):** "Eventbrite alternative", "BookMyShow
  alternative for organizers", "[Ticketmaster/DICE] alternative", "cheapest ticketing
  platform", "sell tickets online [country]".
- **Solution keywords:** "college fest ticketing", "concert ticketing software",
  "cinema booking system", "conference registration platform", "sports ticketing".
- **How-to / TOFU:** "how to run a [fest/concert/conference/comedy night]",
  "how to sell tickets on WhatsApp", "event payout explained", "reduce no-shows".
- **Programmatic:** per-city/segment pages ("event ticketing in {city}").
- **Technical:** fast, structured data (Event schema), clean IA, internal links from
  guides → landing pages → calculator.
- **Local:** per-market domains/subfolders + currency + local competitor comparisons.
- **Target:** rank page-1 for comparison + segment terms in beachhead within 2–3 quarters.

## Customer stories & case studies

Use the [case-study template](../gtm/MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md#case-study-template):
before/after, quantified results (GMV, payment success, payout speed, fee savings,
hours saved), quote. Target **3+ from the pilot**, then 1/month. Gate depth of
detail with the organizer; anonymize where needed.

## Video strategy

- **Hero:** 3-min self-serve demo + 90-sec sizzle for ads/social.
- **Library:** the 10 how-to videos (Support Playbook) for KB + YouTube.
- **Social cuts:** 15–60s tips, "publish in 15 min" speed-runs, customer clips.
- **Owned:** YouTube channel as the SEO + KB backbone.

## Email campaigns

- **Lifecycle** (built-in triggers): welcome/activation, stuck, first-sale, pre-event,
  post-event, at-risk, payout, win-back.
- **Newsletters:** monthly product + case study; segment tips.
- **Nurture:** calculator lead → fee-savings sequence → demo/CTA.

## Referral program

- **Organizer referral:** give fee credit / get fee credit; track referred → activated.
- **Ambassador program:** campus + power organizers earn perks/credits for signups.
- **Case-study/reference flywheel:** every 🟢 organizer → 2 intros (CS-driven).
- **Target:** referral-sourced signups ≥ 20% of new organizers by Q2.

## Launch campaign

Run the [GTM launch campaign](../gtm/MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md#launch-campaign)
("Publish in 15 minutes"): pre-launch waitlist + founding organizers → launch post +
demo + PR/PH + webinar + calculator → amplify via segment SEO + ambassadors +
referral → scale with partner co-marketing.

## Measurement (conversion + acquisition)

| Funnel stage                                                                       | Metric                                         | Target                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Reach                                                                              | organic sessions, brand search, social reach   | ↑                       |
| Acquire                                                                            | site → signup conversion                       | benchmark → ↑           |
| Activate                                                                           | signup → publish (7-day)                       | ≥ 60%                   |
| Convert                                                                            | calculator completions → leads; demo→pilot     | ↑                       |
| Efficiency                                                                         | **CAC by channel**, blended CAC, CAC payback   | payback < 12 mo (→ < 6) |
| Amplify                                                                            | referral-sourced %, case studies published     | ≥ 20% / 1+/mo           |
| Brand                                                                              | share of voice, backlinks, NPS-driven advocacy | ↑                       |
| Attribute by channel + segment + market; hold **paid spend gated** until unit      |
| economics prove out. Full tree: [Success Metrics](../gtm/SUCCESS-METRICS-KPIS.md). |
