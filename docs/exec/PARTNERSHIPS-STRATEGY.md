# VP Partnerships — Partnership Strategy

Partnerships lower CAC, add distribution, and deepen the moat. Extends the
[Acquisition Plan's partner program](../gtm/CUSTOMER-ACQUISITION-PLAN.md#phase-7--partnership-opportunities).

## Partner strategy (thesis)

Three partner archetypes, each with a distinct value exchange:

1. **Distribution partners** (universities, cinema chains, sports leagues, PCOs,
   government) — bring organizers/audiences; we bring the platform.
2. **Ecosystem/technology partners** (payments, cloud, CRM/accounting/POS, WhatsApp
   BSP, RFID/cashless) — reduce switching cost + operational friction.
3. **Demand/monetization partners** (sponsors, hotels, travel) — add revenue and
   attendee value on top of the experience.

**Program tiers:** Referral (10–15% first-year) · Reseller/white-label (wholesale
rate card) · Technology (co-listed integration). Track **partner-sourced GMV ≥ 15%**
by year-end.

## Joint GTM (motions)

- **Payment providers:** co-webinars, "merchants who sell tickets" referral list,
  marketplace listing, joint case studies. _Fastest CAC win._
- **Universities:** MoU → one-department pilot → campus standardization; ambassador
  program + SU partnerships.
- **Cinema chains:** pilot 1–2 screens → chain rollout; co-branded app story.
- **PCOs/event companies:** reseller enablement + white-label demos + rate cards.
- **Sponsors:** sponsorship marketplace pilot on top of high-GMV events.
- **Cloud:** marketplace listing + co-sell + credits to fund pilots.

## Revenue sharing (framework)

| Motion                  | Structure                                                                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Referral                | 10–15% of first-year platform revenue from referred org                        |
| Reseller/white-label    | Wholesale rate card; partner sets end price; annual + setup                    |
| Sponsorship/marketplace | Platform % of sponsorship/marketplace GMV                                      |
| Hotel/travel bundles    | Rev-share on bundled experience GMV                                            |
| Technology integration  | Typically no rev-share; co-marketing + stickiness (or referral if they resell) |

## Integration roadmap (reduce switching cost)

| Phase        | Integrations                                                                                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Now          | Payments (live: Stripe/Razorpay/PayPal/Square); WhatsApp BSP (comms live)                            |
| Next (Q2–Q3) | CRM (HubSpot/Zoho), accounting (QuickBooks/Xero/Zoho Books), Google/Outlook calendar, Zapier         |
| Later        | POS (cinema/venue), RFID/cashless (festivals), streaming, marketing (Mailchimp), analytics (GA/Meta) |
| Platform     | Public API + webhooks + embeddable widget for partner ecosystems                                     |

## Target accounts (illustrative shortlist)

- **Payments:** Razorpay (India), Stripe (UK/AU/US), Square (AU/US) — co-marketing first.
- **Universities:** 3–5 flagship campuses per beachhead market (via ambassadors).
- **Cinemas:** 2–3 regional multiplex/independent groups (India first).
- **PCOs/event companies:** 5 agencies running ≥ 20 events/yr.
- **Sports:** 2 state/city associations or academies.
- **Cloud:** the incumbent provider for marketplace + credits.
  (Maintain a live target-account tracker with owner, stage, and partner-sourced GMV.)

## Outreach templates

**Payment provider (co-marketing):**

> Subject: Merchants who need ticketing — let's send them each other's way
> Hi {name}, ETicketsGo runs events _and_ movies on {provider} across {markets},
> with fast payouts and reconciliation your merchants love. Many of your merchants
> ask for ticketing; many of our organizers need great payments. A referral loop +
> a joint "sell tickets on {provider}" webinar could drive volume for both. 15 min?

**University / SU:**

> Subject: One standard for campus events (fests, alumni, sports)
> Hi {name}, campus teams juggle forms, cash, and Eventbrite fees. ETicketsGo gives
> {university} branded, auditable ticketing — published in 15 minutes, with QR
> check-in and clean finance. We'd love to pilot with one department + your student
> committee (Founding-Organizer perks). Worth a look?

**Cinema chain:**

> Subject: Your own booking app + faster payouts (not the aggregator's)
> Hi {name}, aggregators own your audience and charge high convenience fees.
> ETicketsGo gives {chain} a white-label booking experience, seat maps, faster
> payouts, and your own customer data — piloted on 1–2 screens with zero downside.

**PCO / event company (reseller):**

> Subject: Run all your clients' events on one platform (with your margin)
> Hi {name}, instead of stacking per-event fees across tools, resell ETicketsGo
> white-label to your clients — one platform for tickets, payments, check-in, and
> payouts, with a wholesale rate card that protects your margin. Quick demo?

**Sponsor / brand:**

> Subject: Reach engaged audiences at live experiences — measurably
> Hi {name}, ETicketsGo hosts {segment} experiences with real attendee reach and
> reporting. A sponsored-experience pilot could put {brand} in front of {audience}
> with clean, measurable results. Interested in a test?

## Partnership KPIs

Partner-sourced pipeline + GMV (≥ 15% target), # signed by tier, integrations
shipped, co-marketing leads, reseller ACV, time-to-first-partner-deal. Feed into
[Success Metrics](../gtm/SUCCESS-METRICS-KPIS.md).
