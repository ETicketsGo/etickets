### Defects and findings (most important first)

1. **UAT payout was marked PAID with an unverified bank account** (UAT PAYOUT-6) and **staff could save/replace an
   organizer bank account and edit legal identity** (UAT AZ-1, AZ-4). Cause: UAT runs a build older than #313 and
   #323/#324. QA refuses all of these. Action: deploy UAT before any further UAT certification; do not use UAT
   results as evidence of current behaviour.
2. **FIN-1 (QA, display only):** after refunds, the organizer's `/payouts/summary` and `/payouts/finance` still show
   the full INR 2,996.00 as held/pending, while the admin settlement row (`refundsMinor 191,809`,
   `payableMinor 0`) and the generated payout (gross 299,600 minus refund 199,800 = net 99,800) are correct.
   Settlement and payout are right; the organizer read models overstate what is owed. Needs a fix before pilot
   organizers read those screens.
   Root cause (two separate read rules, both in the frozen Finance workstream, so not changed here):
   - `/payouts/summary` `held`: `PayoutsService.heldRevenue` sums `Booking.subtotalMinor` for every online booking
     of a not-yet-payable event, with no booking-status filter and no refund netting. A fully refunded booking
     stays in `held` until the event becomes payable.
   - `/payouts/finance` `pending`: `buildSummary` in `finance/unified-finance.composer.ts` counts a provider
     settlement's entitlement as `Settlement.grossSalesMinor` (documented contract), so refunds recorded on the
     settlement (`refundsMinor`) do not reduce `pending`. The test organization has no Route account, so this
     settlement can never pay; it still appears as pending next to the platform payout.
   Proposed fix (needs owner approval: it changes certified read-model contracts, not payout execution): net
   COMPLETED refunds out of `held`, show held as gross with a separate refunds figure, and leave a HELD provider
   settlement for an organizer without a linked account out of `pending` (or show it as needing attention).
   Pilot mitigation until then: tell pilot organizers to read the payout row (gross, refunds, net) and not the
   held/pending totals.
3. **UAT payment confirmation is late (about 11 minutes) and comes from the recovery sweeper, not the webhook** (UAT
   PAY-2). QA and UAT share one Razorpay test key id; a Razorpay account has one webhook target, so UAT never gets
   the webhook. The buyer sees "Booking pending" for the whole time.
4. **Refund policy observation:** a refund request on a booking whose tickets were already checked in is accepted
   into organizer review (policy treats CHECKED_IN as refundable). The organizer REJECT left the booking CONFIRMED and
   paid nothing. My first expectation (refuse the request) was wrong; recorded as PASS on the real behaviour.
5. **Provider settlement stays HELD, organizer has no Razorpay Route account** (QA PAYOUT-9): only the platform-path
   payout was exercised. Provider transfers remain off by the owner release gate.
6. UAT stale-build misses: event create is not idempotent (two events), no `/notifications/feed`.

### Blockers (not bypassed)

- Bank verification and PAID need a second authorised staff member; only the super admin exists (QA PAYOUT-7).
- Refund failure at the provider cannot be forced in Razorpay test mode (F-RF-1).
- A genuinely signed webhook delayed or duplicated cannot be built without the webhook secret, which this run must
  not read (F-WH-3). Evidence is indirect: forged confirmations are refused (F-WH-1, F-WH-2), re-paying a CONFIRMED
  booking is refused (F-DUP-1), double refund is refused (F-DUP-2), and double-submit create is idempotent on QA
  (F-DS, F-DS-2).
- Real mailbox receipt of the email (COM-4): only the SES delivery record (status SENT) is proven; buyer addresses
  are `.test`.
- Provider-side reconciliation (REC-3): the discrepancy queue is empty, but this run could not show it compared
  Razorpay's own records. The Razorpay readiness audit's report that QA reconciliation cannot verify 9 payments was
  not independently proven here.
