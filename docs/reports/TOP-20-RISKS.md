# ETicketsGo — Top 20 Remaining Risks

> The 20 most material residual risks at commercial launch, ranked. Drawn from the
> [Tech Debt Register](./TECH-DEBT-REGISTER.md), the
> [Security Validation](./SECURITY-VALIDATION.md), the
> [Capacity Report](./CAPACITY-REPORT.md), the
> [Known Limitations](./KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md), and the
> [Disaster Recovery](./DISASTER-RECOVERY.md) plan. Honest ratings; specific
> mitigations. **Likelihood / Impact:** L / M / H.
>
> Categories: **tech**, **security (sec)**, **ops**, **business (biz)**.

Note: the money/inventory core is atomic + idempotent and verified live (25
concurrent same-seat → exactly 1 wins; 49 GA holds == stock, 0 oversell) — so
"double-charge / oversell" is **not** in this list. These are the risks that
remain.
