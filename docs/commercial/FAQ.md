# ETicketsGo — FAQ

Customer- and organizer-facing answers. Adapt copy for the public help center.

## For customers

**How do I buy tickets?** Browse or search events, open an event, pick your ticket type(s)
and quantity, and check out. You'll see the full total (including any fees) before paying.

**Where are my tickets?** In your account under **Tickets** / **My experiences**. Each ticket
has a secure QR code used for entry. Tickets are available offline once opened.

**Can I use my phone at the gate?** Yes — open the ticket and show the QR. "Event Day Mode"
keeps the screen awake and works even without a signal (it shows the last verified state).

**What fees will I pay?** Depending on the event, a booking fee and payment-processing fee
may apply; the exact total is always shown before payment (see [PLATFORM-FEES.md](PLATFORM-FEES.md)).

**Can I get a refund?** Refunds are available for confirmed bookings up to the event's
refund cut-off (default 48 hours before the session). See the [Refund Policy](REFUND-POLICY.md).

**My payment was declined — what now?** Try another card or payment method. No ticket is
issued and you aren't charged for a declined payment.

**Can I share or transfer a ticket?** Yes — use Share on a ticket to send a view, guest, or
transfer link with an expiry; you can revoke it anytime.

**My booking says "pending".** Payment confirmation can take a moment; the page updates
automatically. If it doesn't confirm shortly, contact support with your booking reference.

## For organizers

**How do I start selling?** Create an account, complete organizer onboarding (including
payment/merchant setup for live payouts), then create your first event.

**How do I set prices and fees?** Add ticket types with prices and choose a fee mode (who
pays fees) in the pricing step (see [PLATFORM-FEES.md](PLATFORM-FEES.md)).

**How do I check attendees in?** Use online check-in in the organizer app, or the offline
gate check-in (pilot) where enabled (see [PILOT-RUNBOOK.md](../guides/PILOT-RUNBOOK.md)).

**When do I get paid?** Proceeds settle to your verified merchant account net of fees,
refunds, and chargebacks (see [Organizer Agreement](ORGANIZER-AGREEMENT.md) and the finance
console).

**Can I run promotions?** Yes — create coupons (percentage or fixed) with limits and expiry.

**Where are my sales analytics?** The organizer dashboard and analytics show sales, revenue,
attendance, and check-in metrics; reports can be exported.
