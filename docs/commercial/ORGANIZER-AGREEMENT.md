This Agreement governs an Organizer's use of ETicketsGo to sell tickets and operate events.
By onboarding, the Organizer accepts these terms in addition to the
[Terms & Conditions](TERMS-AND-CONDITIONS.md).

## 1. Eligibility & onboarding

- The Organizer is a legal entity or individual authorized to sell tickets for their events.
- Payment onboarding (merchant account, KYC, and where applicable sandbox certification)
  must be completed before receiving live payouts (see [MERCHANT-ONBOARDING.md](../guides/MERCHANT-ONBOARDING.md)).

## 2. Listings & honoring tickets

- Listings must be accurate (event, date, venue, price, terms).
- The Organizer will honor all validly issued tickets and operate lawful, safe events.
- The Organizer sets and honors its per-event refund policy (see [Refund Policy](REFUND-POLICY.md)).

## 3. Fees & payouts

- ETicketsGo charges booking and payment-processing fees per [PLATFORM-FEES.md](PLATFORM-FEES.md);
  the Organizer selects the fee mode (who bears fees).
- Payouts settle to the Organizer's verified account per the settlement schedule, net of
  fees, refunds, and chargebacks. [Payout schedule / reserves — to be defined.]

## 4. Data & privacy

- The Organizer receives attendee data solely to operate its events and must comply with
  applicable privacy law as an independent/joint controller [scope with counsel], consistent
  with the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md).

## 5. Compliance & prohibited conduct

- No illegal events, fraud, misrepresentation, or circumvention of platform controls.
- The Organizer is responsible for taxes and permits for its events. [Tax handling — see
  International Readiness.]

## 6. Offline check-in

- Where enabled, offline gate check-in is used per the [Pilot Runbook](../guides/PILOT-RUNBOOK.md);
  the server remains the entry authority and safety controls must not be circumvented.
