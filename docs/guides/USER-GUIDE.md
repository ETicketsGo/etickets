# ETicketsGo — Customer User Guide

Everything an attendee needs to discover events, buy tickets, and get in.

## Finding events
- **Browse** (`/events`): search by title, city, or category; "Load more" pages through
  results.
- **Explore / Home**: trending, this weekend, free events, and categories.
- Open an event to see sessions, ticket types, prices, reviews, and FAQs.

## Buying tickets
1. On an event, pick your **ticket type(s)** and **quantity** (subject to per-order limits).
2. For reserved-seating shows, choose seats on the seat map.
3. **Continue to payment** — you'll see the full total, including any fees, before paying.
4. Complete payment. A short hold protects your seats while you pay (a countdown is shown).
5. On success you land on the **confirmation** page; tickets are issued to your account.
   - If it shows "pending", payment is being confirmed — the page updates automatically.
   - If a payment is **declined**, no ticket is issued and you can try another method.

## Your tickets (wallet)
- Find tickets under **account → Tickets / My experiences** (search + filters).
- Each ticket has a secure **QR code** for entry.
- **Event Day Mode** keeps the screen bright and awake and works **offline** (shows the last
  verified state) — ideal at the gate.
- Add an event to your calendar from the confirmation page.

## Sharing & transfers
Use **Share** on a ticket to send a view, guest, or transfer link with an expiry. You can
**revoke** a share anytime.

## Refunds
Refunds are available for confirmed bookings up to the event's cut-off (default 48 hours
before the session). Request from your booking/tickets page. See the
[Refund Policy](../commercial/REFUND-POLICY.md).

## Account & privacy
Manage your profile under account settings. See the
[Privacy Policy](../commercial/PRIVACY-POLICY.md) for how your data is handled.

## Getting help
Check the [FAQ](../commercial/FAQ.md) or contact support (help center / in-app form).
