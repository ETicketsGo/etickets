# ETicketsGo — Go-To-Market Package

The engineering platform is complete. This package turns it into a business:
maximize **organizer success** and **customer adoption** across India, USA, Canada,
Australia, and the UK.

## What ETicketsGo is (the asset we are selling)

A multi-country, multi-provider ticketing platform: event + movie booking, seat
maps and general admission, QR check-in, runtime-configurable payments
(Stripe / Razorpay / PayPal / Square), refunds, organizer payouts, tiered platform
fees, analytics, a CRM foundation, multi-channel notifications (email / SMS /
WhatsApp / push), an organizer portal, a customer portal, and an admin/ops console.

## Positioning (one line)

**"Sell tickets anywhere, get paid everywhere — in 15 minutes."** The only
ticketing platform that runs your movies _and_ events, routes payments to the right
gateway per country automatically, and pays organizers out without spreadsheets.

## The three bets

1. **Land where incumbents are weak:** mid-market organizers (colleges, clubs,
   community, regional cinemas, conferences) underserved by BookMyShow/Ticketmaster
   scale and overcharged by Eventbrite fees.
2. **Win on payments + payouts:** multi-country routing, failover, reconciliation,
   fast organizer payouts — the operational pain nobody else solves end-to-end.
3. **Convert with time-to-value:** first experience live in <15 minutes; first
   payout within the promised window. Success is the marketing.
