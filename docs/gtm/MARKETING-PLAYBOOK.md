# Marketing Playbook

Covers **Phase 8**: website copy, landing pages, demo script, pitch deck, one-pager,
case study template, ROI calculator, launch campaign.

## Brand + messaging

- **Positioning:** _Sell tickets anywhere, get paid everywhere — in 15 minutes._
- **Category:** "Ticketing + payments for movies **and** events."
- **Voice:** confident, plain-spoken, organizer-first, anti-jargon.
- **Message hierarchy:** (1) time-to-value, (2) payments/payouts, (3) your brand +
  your data, (4) movies **and** events, (5) transparent fees.

## Demo script

Use the [Sales Playbook demo script](./SALES-PLAYBOOK.md#demo-script-1520-min)

## Pitch deck (12 slides)

1. Title + one-liner. 2. The problem (fees, payout pain, fragmented tools, aggregator
   lock-in). 3. Why now (multi-country events + movies boom; fee fatigue). 4. The
   product (movies+events, one platform). 5. Live demo / product shots. 6. The moat
   (payments routing + payouts + reconciliation + time-to-value). 7. Market (India +
   US/CA/AU/UK; TAM/SAM/SOM). 8. Competition + our wedge. 9. Business model + pricing +
   unit economics. 10. Traction (pilot results / case studies). 11. GTM + partnerships.
2. Team + ask/roadmap.

## One-pager (leave-behind)

Header (logo + one-liner) · Problem (3 bullets) · Solution (3 bullets) · Key
features (movies+events, payments in 5 countries, seat maps + QR, refunds+payouts,
analytics, white-label) · Differentiators · Proof (metrics/quote) · Pricing summary
· CTA + contact + QR to demo.

## Case study template

- **Organizer + segment + market.**
- **Before:** tools, fees, payout lag, pain (quantified).
- **Why ETicketsGo.**
- **Rollout:** setup time, first event.
- **Results:** GMV, tickets, payment success %, payout speed, no-show/refund change,
  hours saved, fee savings.
- **Quote.** **What's next.** (One-page + web version.)

## ROI / fee calculator (interactive)

Inputs: tickets/yr, avg price, current provider + fee, payout lag, no-show rate.
Outputs:

- **Fee savings** vs current (per event + annual).
- **Faster cash** (payout-days × avg float).
- **No-show reduction value** (reminders/waitlist).
- **Hours saved** (setup + reconciliation) × loaded rate.
- **Net annual benefit + payback.** Emailable PDF → lead capture; embed on
  `/eventbrite-alternative`. (Model mirrors the fee-rule + payout logic in-product.)

## Launch campaign (beachhead: India + one English market)

**Theme:** "Publish in 15 minutes."

| Phase          | Weeks | Plays                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pre-launch** | −4→0  | Waitlist + "founding organizers" (pilot); teaser content; partner (Razorpay/campus) co-announce; 10 pilot logos lined up                                                     |
| **Launch**     | 0→2   | Launch post + demo video + Product Hunt/press; pilot case studies; fee-calculator campaign; webinar "Ditch the aggregator"                                                   |
| **Amplify**    | 2→8   | Segment landing pages + SEO ("Eventbrite alternative", "BookMyShow alternative for organizers"); ambassador program; referral engine; paid retargeting only after ROI proven |
| **Scale**      | 8+    | US/CA content; partnership co-marketing; customer-story flywheel                                                                                                             |

**Content engine:** persona quick-starts, "how to run a [fest/concert/conference]"
guides, fee-comparison pieces, payout-explainer, security/trust posts. Repurpose
pilot data into proof.

**Channels:** SEO + comparison content (long tail), founder LinkedIn/X, campus +
community, WhatsApp/Instagram (India), partner co-marketing, webinars, targeted
paid **after** unit economics validated.

## Marketing KPIs

Site → signup conversion, signup → publish (activation), CAC by channel, calculator
completions → leads, landing-page conversion by segment, referral rate, brand search
volume. Targets in [Success Metrics](./SUCCESS-METRICS-KPIS.md).
