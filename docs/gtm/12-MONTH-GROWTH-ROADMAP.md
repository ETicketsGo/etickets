# 12-Month Growth Roadmap

Covers **Phase 10** as a growth arc. Picks up after the
[90-Day Launch Plan](./90-DAY-LAUNCH-PLAN.md). Growth = **more organizers × more
experiences × more depth**, protected by guardrails (payment success, refunds,
Sev-1, CSAT). **No architecture redesign; no speculative backend** — growth levers
are GTM + packaging + light product polish that already-shipped capabilities enable.

## Arc at a glance

| Quarter         | Theme  | Motion                                      | Milestone                         |
| --------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Q1 (M1–3)**   | Prove  | Pilot → GA in beachhead                     | 10 orgs → GA; 3 case studies      |
| **Q2 (M4–6)**   | Repeat | Retention + partnerships + self-serve scale | 250–500 active orgs; repeat ≥ 45% |
| **Q3 (M7–9)**   | Expand | US/Canada entry; segment depth              | new-market beachhead; 500+ orgs   |
| **Q4 (M10–12)** | Scale  | Flywheel + white-label + partner GMV        | 1,000+ orgs; partner GMV ≥ 15%    |

## Quarter-by-quarter

### Q1 — Prove (Months 1–3)

- Execute the 90-day plan; reach GA in India + one English market.
- Nail activation (<15-min publish), payment reliability, payouts on-time.
- **Exit:** GA live, references in hand, guardrails green.

### Q2 — Repeat (Months 4–6)

**Focus: retention + acquisition efficiency.**

- **Retention:** duplicate-event + recurring shows + seasonal templates; health-score
  save plays; QBRs for managed accounts; win-back for dormant.
- **Acquisition:** self-serve funnel optimization; SEO comparison content; ambassador
  - referral engine at scale; partner co-sell (payments, campus, PCO).
- **Packaging:** finalize Standard/Pro/Business tiers + nonprofit/education discount;
  ship the **white-label** motion for event companies (reseller rate cards).
- **Targets:** 250–500 active organizers; repeat organizers ≥ 45%; NRR ≥ 100%;
  CAC payback trending < 9 mo.

### Q3 — Expand (Months 7–9)

**Focus: new markets + segment depth (only where we have proof).**

- **US/Canada entry** with references + partnerships (Stripe co-marketing, PCOs,
  universities); localize pricing/receipts/tax messaging.
- **Segment depth:** cinema chain rollouts, conference/music promoter references,
  sports season packages, sponsor marketplace pilots.
- **Product polish (non-speculative):** advanced analytics/reporting surfacing,
  richer discounts/coupons UX, embeddable widgets, integrations (CRM/accounting/POS)
  that reduce switching cost.
- **Targets:** 500+ active organizers; beachhead + first expansion-market run-rate;
  logo retention ≥ 85%.

### Q4 — Scale (Months 10–12)

**Focus: flywheel + partner-led GMV + margin.**

- **Flywheel:** every happy organizer → case study + 2 referrals; attendee → repeat
  attendee via CRM + recommendations.
- **Partnerships:** partner-sourced GMV ≥ 15%; integration marketplace; sponsor
  marketplace live.
- **White-label / enterprise:** multiplex + large event-company deals; SLAs; SSO.
- **Margin:** optimize support cost (deflection ≥ 50%), payment routing, and take
  rate stability.
- **Targets:** 1,000+ active organizers; 10,000+ experiences; scale GMV run-rate;
  NRR ≥ 110%; gross margin trending to SaaS norms.

## Growth levers (ranked)

1. **Time-to-value** (activation) — biggest lever on every downstream metric.
2. **Retention/repeat** — repeat organizers compound cheaply; seasonal + duplicate.
3. **Partnerships** — payments + campus + cinema + PCO channels lower CAC.
4. **References/flywheel** — case studies → self-serve conversion.
5. **Packaging** — Pro/white-label/nonprofit capture willingness-to-pay without new backend.
6. **Multi-market** — reuse the same platform (payments already multi-country) for new TAM.

## Guardrails (hold every quarter)

Payment success ≥ 97% · refund rate ≤ segment median · 0 unresolved Sev-1 · CSAT ≥ 4.3
· NRR ≥ 100% · payouts on-time. Growth that breaches a guardrail pauses until fixed.

## Operating cadence

Weekly (ops/CS), monthly (business review), quarterly (board + QBRs + roadmap
voting). All metrics per [Success Metrics & KPIs](./SUCCESS-METRICS-KPIS.md).

## What we will NOT do (discipline)

- No architecture redesign; no speculative backend features.
- No premature US paid-CAC before beachhead unit economics prove out.
- No growth that sacrifices money-integrity or reliability guardrails.
- No feature sprawl — every build must map to activation, retention, or a signed deal.
