# Customer Acquisition Plan

Covers **Phase 1 (personas)** and **Phase 7 (partnerships)**. Personas drive
targeting, messaging (see [Sales Playbook](./SALES-PLAYBOOK.md)), and the pilot
[shortlist](./PILOT-PROGRAM-GUIDE.md).

## Segment priority (where to start)

| Tier                    | Segments                                                                                 | Why first                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A — beachhead**       | Colleges/Universities, Event Companies, Conference/Music/Comedy organizers, Sports Clubs | Frequent events, fast decisions, painful status quo (spreadsheets/free tools), reference-able |
| **B — expand**          | Regional cinemas/multiplex, Wedding/Event planners, Startup communities, Nonprofits      | Higher value or higher volume; longer cycles                                                  |
| **C — land-and-anchor** | Corporate HR, Religious orgs, Government event departments                               | Big logos, procurement-heavy; pursue via partnerships/references                              |

## Personas

> Format per persona: **Problems · Current Workflow · Pain Points · Buying Decision
> · Decision Makers · Budget · Expected ROI.**

### 1. Movie Theatre Owners (single/independent)

- **Problems:** Empty weekday seats; dependence on aggregators that own the customer and take high convenience fees; no direct customer data.
- **Current Workflow:** Walk-in box office + one aggregator (BookMyShow/District); manual show scheduling; cash + card POS.
- **Pain Points:** Aggregator fees + payout delays; zero CRM; can't run promos to own audience; reconciliation by hand.
- **Buying Decision:** Trigger = fee hikes or a new screen; evaluate on payout speed, own-branding, and per-ticket economics.
- **Decision Makers:** Owner / GM (also the buyer).
- **Budget:** Low fixed; fee-based tolerance ~1–3% if payouts are faster and data is theirs.
- **Expected ROI:** +8–15% weekday occupancy via own-audience promos; 1–2 days faster cash; recovered convenience-fee margin.

### 2. Multiplex Chains (regional, 3–30 screens)

- **Problems:** Fragmented tooling across sites; want their own app/brand, not just aggregator listings; loyalty + F&B upsell.
- **Current Workflow:** Central booking vendor + aggregators; POS per site; manual settlement across screens.
- **Pain Points:** Vendor lock-in; slow feature velocity; multi-site reconciliation; no unified analytics.
- **Buying Decision:** RFP or referral; evaluate white-label, seat-map fidelity, settlement/reporting, SLA.
- **Decision Makers:** COO / Head of Operations + Finance; IT sign-off.
- **Budget:** SaaS + per-ticket; ₹ lakhs/yr or mid-5-figures USD; will pay for white-label + SLA.
- **Expected ROI:** Consolidated ops; own-brand app conversion; faster multi-site settlement; analytics-driven programming.

### 3. College Administrators (fests, cultural/technical events)

- **Problems:** Fest ticketing on WhatsApp/Google Forms; cash handling; gate chaos; no accountability.
- **Current Workflow:** Forms + UPI/Venmo screenshots; manual attendee lists; paper/rubber-stamp entry.
- **Pain Points:** Payment leakage/fraud; duplicate entries; no refunds; finance can't audit.
- **Buying Decision:** Fast (weeks); champion = student committee; needs faculty/finance nod.
- **Decision Makers:** Student council lead + faculty coordinator + finance office.
- **Budget:** Very low; attendee-paid booking fee preferred; grants/sponsorship possible.
- **Expected ROI:** Zero cash leakage; QR check-in kills duplicates; auditable finance; reusable every semester.

### 4. Universities (department + central events, alumni, sports)

- **Problems:** Many departments, no standard; alumni/donor events need branding + receipts; access control.
- **Current Workflow:** Mix of Eventbrite, forms, box office; siloed data; manual comps/invites.
- **Pain Points:** No central control/reporting; inconsistent brand; tax receipts; role-based access.
- **Buying Decision:** Procurement-led; pilot one department then standardize; security/privacy review.
- **Decision Makers:** Events/Advancement office + Procurement + IT/Security.
- **Budget:** Annual contract (4–5 figures USD); multi-department justifies platform fee.
- **Expected ROI:** One standard = lower admin cost; better alumni data; branded, compliant receipts.

### 5. Sports Clubs (local leagues, academies, semi-pro)

- **Problems:** Season + match-day tickets, memberships, limited-capacity venues; season-ticket renewals.
- **Current Workflow:** Cash/UPI at gate; WhatsApp groups; spreadsheets for members.
- **Pain Points:** Gate queues; no season/bundle tickets; no member CRM; refunds on rain-offs.
- **Buying Decision:** Season start trigger; evaluate speed at gate, bundles, member data.
- **Decision Makers:** Club secretary / operations manager; treasurer.
- **Budget:** Low-mid; per-ticket + optional membership module fee.
- **Expected ROI:** Faster gates; higher renewal via CRM; fewer no-shows; clean rain-off refunds.

### 6. Event Companies (agencies running many clients' events)

- **Problems:** Run 10s–100s of events/yr for clients; need multi-brand, fast setup, reliable payouts, reporting per client.
- **Current Workflow:** A patchwork of Eventbrite/DICE/Ticketmaster per event; manual client reconciliation.
- **Pain Points:** Per-event fee stacking; slow setup; payout/settlement per client; client-facing reporting.
- **Buying Decision:** ROI + reliability; want partner/reseller terms; power-user features.
- **Decision Makers:** Founder/Ops Director; Finance.
- **Budget:** Volume-based; negotiates rate cards; highest LTV segment.
- **Expected ROI:** Faster turnarounds, margin on fees, one tool for all clients, white-label per client.

### 7. Wedding / Event Planners (private, invite-only)

- **Problems:** RSVP + guest management, meal counts, plus-ones, private access, cashless sub-events.
- **Current Workflow:** Paper invites + WhatsApp RSVPs + spreadsheets.
- **Pain Points:** No headcount accuracy; gate-crashing; no cashless vendor stalls; last-minute changes.
- **Buying Decision:** Per-event; referral-driven; ease + elegance matter more than price.
- **Decision Makers:** Planner (buyer) on behalf of client.
- **Budget:** Per-event fee absorbed into package; premium tolerable.
- **Expected ROI:** Accurate headcount = catering savings; private QR access; premium guest experience.

### 8. Corporate HR Teams (town halls, offsites, L&D, fests)

- **Problems:** Internal + family events, RSVP, capacity, dietary, cost-center reporting; sometimes paid family days.
- **Current Workflow:** Outlook/Google + forms; manual approvals; finance reconciliation by cost center.
- **Pain Points:** No capacity control; poor reporting; badge/check-in; vendor onboarding/security.
- **Buying Decision:** Procurement + security review; annual tool budget; needs SSO/privacy.
- **Decision Makers:** HR Ops + Procurement + IT Security.
- **Budget:** Annual SaaS; mid 4–5 figures; will pay for compliance.
- **Expected ROI:** Time saved per event; accurate reporting; professional employee experience.

### 9. Startup Communities (meetups, demo days, hackathons)

- **Problems:** Frequent free/low-cost events, waitlists, sponsors, recurring series, community CRM.
- **Current Workflow:** Meetup.com / Luma / Google Forms; sponsor asks ad hoc.
- **Pain Points:** No-shows on free events; sponsor ROI reporting; recurring setup; data ownership.
- **Buying Decision:** Fast, self-serve; price-sensitive; loves modern UX + API.
- **Decision Makers:** Community manager / founder.
- **Budget:** Freemium; converts on volume or paid events.
- **Expected ROI:** Lower no-shows (reminders + waitlist), sponsor reporting, owned community data.

### 10. Religious Organizations (festivals, darshan/slots, donations)

- **Problems:** Capacity/slotting for high-demand days, crowd control, donations + receipts, member comms.
- **Current Workflow:** Physical tokens/queues; cash donations; volunteer-run gates.
- **Pain Points:** Overcrowding safety; donation receipts/compliance; language/accessibility; volunteer training.
- **Buying Decision:** Trust + references; committee-led; often relationship/partnership driven.
- **Decision Makers:** Trust board / management committee; treasurer.
- **Budget:** Low; often subsidized; donation-linked.
- **Expected ROI:** Safer crowd control via slots + QR; compliant donation receipts; better member comms.

### 11. Nonprofits (galas, fundraisers, awareness events)

- **Problems:** Ticketed fundraisers + donations, sponsor tiers, tax receipts, tight budgets.
- **Current Workflow:** Eventbrite + separate donation tool + spreadsheets.
- **Pain Points:** Fees eat donations; split tools; receipts/compliance; volunteer usability.
- **Buying Decision:** Cost + mission fit; board approval; nonprofit pricing expected.
- **Decision Makers:** ED / Development Director; Board treasurer.
- **Budget:** Very cost-sensitive; expects discount/nonprofit tier.
- **Expected ROI:** More net donations (lower fees, upsell), one tool, compliant receipts.

### 12. Government Event Departments (public events, civic programs)

- **Problems:** Public-scale events, free/paid, accessibility, transparency, procurement + audit.
- **Current Workflow:** Tenders to vendors per event; manual crowd management; paper trails.
- **Pain Points:** Procurement cycles; accessibility/compliance; auditability; multi-language; data residency.
- **Buying Decision:** Formal tender/empanelment; references + compliance are gating.
- **Decision Makers:** Department head + Procurement + IT/Security; political sign-off.
- **Budget:** Project/annual; large but slow; compliance-priced.
- **Expected ROI:** Transparent, auditable public ticketing; safer crowds; accessibility compliance.

### 13. Conference Organizers (professional/industry, multi-day, multi-track)

- **Problems:** Tiers (early-bird/group/VIP), tracks/sessions, badges, sponsors/exhibitors, invoicing.
- **Current Workflow:** Eventbrite/Hopin/Cvent + badge vendor + invoicing tool.
- **Pain Points:** Fee stacking; complex tiers/discounts; badge + on-site check-in; sponsor/exhibitor mgmt; B2B invoicing.
- **Buying Decision:** Feature-fit + reliability; annual or per-conference; demo-driven.
- **Decision Makers:** Conference director / producer; Finance for invoicing.
- **Budget:** Mid-high; per-ticket + modules; pays for reliability at scale.
- **Expected ROI:** Higher early-bird conversion; smooth on-site check-in; sponsor value; consolidated tooling.

### 14. Music Organizers (concerts, festivals, clubs, tours)

- **Problems:** High-demand on-sales, tiers/allocations, anti-scalping, cashless, multi-day festivals.
- **Current Workflow:** DICE/Ticketmaster/Skiddle/BookMyShow; separate cashless RFID vendor.
- **Pain Points:** On-sale spikes/reliability; scalping; payout timing; fan data ownership; refunds on cancellation.
- **Buying Decision:** Reliability + fan experience + economics; promoter references decisive.
- **Decision Makers:** Promoter / festival director; Finance.
- **Budget:** Volume-based; per-ticket; high GMV.
- **Expected ROI:** Reliable on-sales, less scalping, faster payouts, owned fan data, upsell.

### 15. Comedy Organizers (clubs, tours, open mics)

- **Problems:** Recurring shows, small venues, last-minute, dynamic pricing, no-shows.
- **Current Workflow:** Instagram DMs + Google Forms + cash at door.
- **Pain Points:** No-shows; door cash handling; recurring show setup; fan CRM/repeat.
- **Buying Decision:** Fast, cheap, self-serve; loves mobile-first + repeat automation.
- **Decision Makers:** Comedian / club manager.
- **Budget:** Very low; attendee booking fee; converts on repeat volume.
- **Expected ROI:** Fewer no-shows, no door cash, one-tap recurring setup, repeat-fan revenue.

## Acquisition channels (by segment)

| Channel                      | Best for                                       | Motion                       |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Founder-led outbound + demos | Event companies, conferences, multiplex, music | 1:1, reference-driven        |
| Campus ambassadors           | Colleges/universities, startup communities     | Student reps + faculty intro |
| Self-serve + content         | Comedy, startup meetups, small nonprofits      | Product-led, SEO, templates  |
| Partnerships (Phase 7)       | Cinemas, sports, government, corporate         | Channel + co-sell            |
| Referrals + case studies     | All                                            | Every happy pilot → 2 intros |

## Phase 7 — Partnership opportunities

| Partner type             | Targets                                                                     | Value exchange                                                      | Motion                                 |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Universities**         | Student affairs, alumni/advancement offices, sports depts                   | Standardized campus ticketing; ambassador program                   | MoU + department pilot → campus-wide   |
| **Cinema chains**        | Regional multiplexes, single-screen associations                            | White-label booking + payouts; co-branded app                       | Pilot 1–2 screens → chain rollout      |
| **Sports organizations** | State/city associations, academies, leagues                                 | Season ticketing + member CRM                                       | League-level deal → club rollout       |
| **Event companies**      | Agencies, PCOs, promoters                                                   | **Reseller/partner** rate cards; white-label per client             | Partner tier + co-sell                 |
| **Payment providers**    | Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal, Square, local acquirers                           | Co-marketing; referral of merchants needing ticketing; better rates | Joint webinars, marketplace listing    |
| **Sponsors/brands**      | Consumer brands, F&B, fintech                                               | Sponsored experiences, data-light audience reach                    | Sponsorship marketplace on-platform    |
| **Government**           | Tourism boards, city event depts, cultural ministries                       | Empanelment for public events; accessibility compliance             | Tender + reference sites               |
| **Technology partners**  | POS, RFID/cashless, CRM (HubSpot/Zoho), streaming, WhatsApp BSP, accounting | Integrations that reduce switching cost                             | Integration marketplace + co-marketing |

**Partner program tiers:** Referral (10–15% first-year), Reseller/white-label
(wholesale rate card), Technology (co-listed integration). Track partner-sourced GMV
as a first-class KPI ([metrics](./SUCCESS-METRICS-KPIS.md)).
