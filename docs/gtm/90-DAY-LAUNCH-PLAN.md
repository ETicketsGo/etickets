# 90-Day Launch Plan

Covers **Phase 4 + Phase 10** as a time-boxed plan. Assumes the platform is
complete. Beachhead: **India + one English market (UK or AU)**.

## Objectives (by Day 90)

- Pilot complete: **10 organizers · 100 experiences · 5,000 attendees** processed.
- **3+ case studies**, **NPS ≥ 40**, **payment success ≥ 97%**, **0 open Sev-1**.
- **Self-serve GA live** in the beachhead with the launch campaign running.
- **2 partnerships** signed (e.g., a payment provider co-marketing + a campus/PCO).

## Workstreams & owners

| Workstream                | Owner             | North star                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Pilot & Customer Success  | VP CS             | activation, CSAT, references             |
| Product/Onboarding polish | CPO               | <15-min publish, top friction fixed      |
| Sales & Partnerships      | Head of Sales Eng | pilots→commit, 2 partners                |
| Marketing & Content       | Marketing Dir     | site, calculator, campaign, case studies |
| Ops & Reliability         | Launch Mgr        | 0 Sev-1, readiness, payouts on-time      |

## Month 1 — Prepare & recruit (Days 1–30)

**Goal: pilot cohort live and publishing.**

- Recruit + sign **10 Founding Organizers** (diverse per [Pilot Guide](./PILOT-PROGRAM-GUIDE.md)).
- Stand up metrics dashboard (activation, GMV, payment success, refunds, CSAT).
- Ship onboarding polish: use-case picker, persona templates, "go live later" for payments.
- Build launch assets: home + 3 landing pages, ROI/fee calculator, demo videos #1–3, one-pager, pitch deck.
- CSM per organizer; onboarding calls; event-day on-call rota.
- Payments: verify each pilot's provider path; readiness/launch-gate green before any live event.
- **Exit M1:** ≥ 7/10 published; first real sales; support rhythm running.

## Month 2 — Run the pilot (Days 31–60)

**Goal: hit pilot scale + generate proof.**

- Drive to **100 experiences / 5,000 attendees**; weekly reviews + digests.
- Event-day operations on every large event; incident reviews within 24h.
- Weekly you-asked-we-shipped releases; drain reconciliation queue weekly.
- Capture **3+ case studies** + NPS/CSAT; collect referrals.
- Marketing: publish first case study, "publish in 15 min" content, waitlist for GA.
- Partnerships: progress payment-provider co-marketing + campus/PCO intro.
- **Exit M2:** success criteria trending to target; case studies drafted.

## Month 3 — Launch to market (Days 61–90)

**Goal: GA + campaign + first partnerships.**

- **Pilot exit go/no-go** (~Day 60–65). If GO → flip **self-serve GA** in beachhead.
- Run the **launch campaign** (Marketing Playbook): launch post + demo video +
  PH/press + webinar "Ditch the aggregator" + fee-calculator campaign + segment SEO.
- Turn on **referral engine** + ambassador program; pilots → referrals.
- Sales: convert pilots to paid; open self-serve→sales handoff; work 2 partner deals to signature.
- Ops: publish status page; confirm payout SLAs; monitor guardrails.
- **Exit M3 (Day 90):** GA live, campaign running, ≥ 6 pilots retained + paying,
  2 partnerships signed, pipeline building.

## Weekly rhythm (every week, all 90 days)

Mon internal review (metrics + incidents + actions) · midweek per-organizer check-ins
· Fri cohort digest + release notes. Track **actions-closed 100%**.

## Risks & mitigations

| Risk                       | Mitigation                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Slow pilot recruitment     | Over-recruit 15 to land 10; partner/ambassador intros; Founding-Organizer perks |
| Event-day payment incident | Readiness gate, failover/maintenance controls, on-call, rollback                |
| Onboarding still >15 min   | Ruthless friction removal in M1; templates; defer live payments                 |
| Low activation             | Lifecycle nudges + CSM outreach + "publish for you" concierge                   |
| Thin references            | Bake case-study capture into weekly reviews; incentivize                        |

## Exit criteria (Day 90 = ready to scale)

- GA live + campaign running; guardrails green.
- ≥ 6 retained pilots; ≥ 3 case studies; NPS ≥ 40; payment success ≥ 97%; 0 open Sev-1.
- 2 partnerships signed; self-serve funnel converting; pipeline for Month 4+.
  → Proceed to the [12-Month Growth Roadmap](./12-MONTH-GROWTH-ROADMAP.md).
