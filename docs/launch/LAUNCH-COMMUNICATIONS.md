# ETicketsGo — Launch Communications Plan

Who we tell what, when. Templates are starting points — adapt tone/branding and clear with
marketing/legal before sending.

## Audiences

- **Pilot organizers** — direct, high-touch (named contact).
- **Pilot customers** — transactional + light announcement.
- **Internal team** — readiness, on-call, status.
- **(Later) Public / GA** — broader marketing, held until after a successful pilot review.

## Timeline

| When              | Audience              | Message                                                           |
| ----------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T-1 week          | Pilot organizers      | Onboarding welcome + what to expect + support contact.            |
| T-1 week          | Internal              | Go-live plan, on-call rota, escalation, dashboards.               |
| Launch day        | Pilot organizers      | "You're live" + monitoring + how to reach us.                     |
| During pilot      | Customers             | Standard booking/ticket transactional emails (already automated). |
| Incident (if any) | Affected parties      | Per [INCIDENT-RESPONSE](INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md) templates.          |
| Post-pilot        | Organizers + internal | Thank-you, results summary, next steps.                           |
| GA (later)        | Public                | Marketing launch — gated on pilot success.                        |

## Templates

**Organizer welcome:** "Welcome to ETicketsGo! Your account is ready. Here's how to create
your first event, set pricing, and reach support. Your point of contact is [name]."

**You're live:** "Your event [name] is live on ETicketsGo. We're monitoring closely — reach
[contact] anytime. Good luck!"

**Post-pilot summary:** "Thank you for piloting with us. Here's how it went: [tickets sold,
check-in rate, settlement]. Next: [rollout plan]."

## Channels

Email (transactional already automated), direct organizer contact, internal chat, status page
(if used). Keep customer-facing legal links (T&C/Privacy/Refund) in the footer + checkout.

## Guardrails

- No public GA announcement until the [post-launch review](POST-LAUNCH-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md)
  passes.
- All external copy reviewed by marketing/legal.
- Never promise capabilities behind off-by-default flags (offline check-in, live payments)
  until certified for that organizer.
