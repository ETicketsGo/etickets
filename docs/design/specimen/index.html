2 / Foundations

Colour is information, not decoration

Three brand colours and four status pairs. Each one has a job and a list of things it may not do.

Paper #FBFAF7
CanvasThe page. Cards are white on it; nothing else is tinted. Never pure white as a page.
Ink #171412
InkText, and the primary action on paper. 17.5:1. Never softened to grey for style.
Marigold #FFBA1A
Marigold, the lightThe action on a stage; on paper a fill with ink on it. Never text on paper, never a warning.
Stage #110F0D
Stage toneA band, not a dark mode: hero, film header, seat map, footer.

Rules the slices follow
  • One primary action per view. Ink on paper, marigold on a stage.
  • Two surfaces and a hairline. One shadow, for things that float.
  • Display face for titles only. Every number is Inter, tabular.
  • A photo or the set title. Never initials, never a stock icon tile.
  • Words for every state. Colour only confirms them.
  • Dates show the venue's own time and zone.
